Narration capture

Records the Ratchet demo narration using this computer’s own speech engine, then sends it back for muxing.

  1. Press Record the narration.
  2. Chrome asks what to share. Choose Entire Screen (not a tab), make sure Share system audio is ON, press Share.
  3. That is all. Leave it completely alone for about 90 seconds — do not press “Stop sharing” and do not switch apps. It says DONE when the upload is finished.

If it says SILENT, the system-audio switch was off. Press the button again and turn it on.
